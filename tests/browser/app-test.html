Take command of your brig and make your fortune on the open sea.

How to play
  • Open sea (online): sign in with Google to save your progress, or sail as a guest. Other captains sail the same sea. Sink them for gold, or team up against the pirates. Harbours are safe from everyone. Explore a large sea of islands. Pirate ships roam and attack when you come close. Dock at any of the three ports to repair and shop; harbours are safe. A boss ship appears every few minutes. If you sink, you return to port with your upgrades but lose half the gold you carry. Your progress is saved on this device.
  • Wave battle: survive wave after wave in a small bay, shop between waves, boss every 5 waves.
  • Drag on the left of the screen to steer. Hold Fire to shoot.
  • The button above Fire switches between your front cannon and 3 cannons on each side. Side cannons are unlocked in the shop or with a purple power-up.
  • Blue lights give speed. Orange lights give rapid fire. Purple lights give side cannons for a while.
  • Ranks and ships: sinking ships earns experience. Rank up from Deckhand to Admiral to unlock bigger ships in the harbour's Shipyard, then buy them with gold. Bigger ships carry more hull and cannons but are slower and turn less sharply. Open sea and Wave battle each keep their own rank and ships.
  • Ship & colours: choose which of your ships to sail, paint the hull, trim, sails and flag, and pick an emblem or draw your own. Do it from the main menu or in any harbour (the Paint tab). More colours and emblems unlock as you rank up.
  • A referee checks every hit between captains, so cheating gets you removed from online play.
  • Tap Menu at the top to pause, turn the music on or off, or go back to the main menu to switch between Open sea and Wave battle.
