What's on board

A whole sea of adventure

Quick battles when you have five minutes, a living world when you have an evening.

⚓

A shared open sea

Sail with captains from around the world. Sink pirates for gold, duel rivals or team up. Harbours are safe havens to repair, upgrade and buy bigger ships.

💥

Real naval combat

Fire your bow cannons or turn and unleash a full broadside. Grab power-ups for speed, rapid fire and side cannons. A fair-play referee checks every hit.

🐙

The kraken rises

Every few minutes the kraken surfaces and every captain online can join the fight. Dig up buried treasure, raid convoys and dodge fire ships.

🏴‍☠️

Fleets, friends & duels

Found a fleet, fly its flag and battle other fleets to hold the island ports. Challenge any captain to a fair 1-on-1 duel in the arena.

🎨

Your ship, your colours

Paint your hull, sails and flag, pick an emblem or draw your own, and show off figureheads, pets, glowing wakes and fireball shots.

🌦️

A living sea

Day turns to night, storms roll in with lightning and towering waves, fog drifts over the islands and gulls cry over the beaches.

📖

Play solo too

Five story missions, endless Wave battles with a boss every five waves, and a quick first voyage that teaches you everything.

🏆

Something new every day

Daily chests and quests, weekly and season leaderboards, achievements and titles, plus weekend events like Double Gold and Kraken Weekend.
